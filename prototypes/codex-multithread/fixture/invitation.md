# Invitation to Tender

The employer invites a lump-sum tender for a small reinforced-concrete utility
building in Cairo, Egypt.

- Submission deadline: 17 September 2026 at 14:00 Africa/Cairo.
- Required technical return: construction method and preliminary programme.
- Required commercial return: priced bill of quantities and basis of estimate.
- Clarifications must be submitted through the employer's formal RFI channel.
- The structural specification calls for concrete grade C35/45.
