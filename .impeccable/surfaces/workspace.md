# Surface brief: tender workspace (Understand first)

Scope and mode: Operate. First surface of the replacement world: the Understand view (manager + documents register at `/tenders/:tenderId/manager` and `/documents`).

Audience, job, proof, constraints: construction field engineers, laptop first then tablet. Job: open a tender package, see what was found and what is missing, open any line with its source, check it, and always see the one next action. Proof is real: artifact locators, versions, hashes, and proposed-vs-accepted kept separate. Constraints: fingerprinted engineer approvals, `~/.quantix` home, plain engineering language, advanced controls in More options, light default plus dark, keyboard and touch.

Chosen direction and memorable moment: Takeoff ledger + stamp rail (roll 277fb5c8, assigned candidate 3 of 7). Moment: stamp a line CHECKED and watch the next action advance.

Unresolved: demo package shape for first run, default density, tablet breakpoints.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The workspace is a ruled takeoff ledger, not a chat app; every row is a claim with its source and stamp state, and the page refuses dashboard cards and chatbot heroes.

OWN-WORLD: Paper ground, carbon rows, hairline rules, one tabular sans with mono locators; a single vermilion stamp accent plus green confirm; stamp chits and a tick rail carry state everywhere.

STORY: The engineer understands the package in minutes, trusts each line because its evidence sits beside it, and stamps decisions that move exactly one next action forward.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Title strip (package, revision, found/missing counts); central ruled contents table with source and stamp per row; right rail with the one next action and evidence strip; Manager note beside the open sheet, office expandable.

FORM: Grounded candidate 3 of 7, seed 277fb5c8; raised with Storm-scale type, Specimen honesty, Datamatics density; timetable, tensegrity and teletext judged competitive and held as alternates.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
