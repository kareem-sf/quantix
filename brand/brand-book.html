QS Mind

The commercial mind of construction. One house and three tools that follow the way a quantity surveyor works: estimate, tender, procure. Three marks are cut from the same ribbon, Tawreed speaks Arabic in Thmanyah's ت, and every brand wears its own colour. Founded and developed by Kareem Safwat.

The family

One ribbonQS Mind, Quanta and Quantix: width 117, outer corners r163, every diagonal and cut at 45°, a 26-unit gap where one piece passes another. Tawreed: the Thmanyah Serif Display Bold ت, outlined.
One accent pieceEach mark has one separate piece that carries the idea: the gold cap, the pulled-out corner, the under-stroke, the ت's two dots.
Own colourMetals of construction. Gold belongs to QS Mind's cap; each product wears its colour on the whole ribbon.
