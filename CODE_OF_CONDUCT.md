# Code of Conduct

Quantix welcomes professional technical collaboration.

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Critique ideas and implementation choices rather than people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, doxxing, or publication of another person's confidential information is not acceptable.

Keep technical discussions relevant to the repository and provide evidence for consequential claims where practical. Do not use project channels to expose confidential Tender data, credentials, or other secrets.

Repository maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors, users, or the project.

Report sensitive conduct or security matters through an appropriate private channel rather than publishing private information.
