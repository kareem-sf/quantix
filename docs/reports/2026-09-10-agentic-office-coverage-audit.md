# Coverage audit of the three original office documents

This audit answers the engineer's question about what was not taken into consideration. The earlier one-word “No” conflated consideration with implementation. The research and concept were considered, and the master plan maps the broad scope. The actual shortfall is incomplete delivery and insufficient item-level execution/acceptance detail, not wholesale omission of those documents.

Read against the current working source on 10 September 2026. This is a document/source audit; no new provider execution, customer-data mutation or test run was performed. A generic Manager may reason about some of these outcomes already. A prompt, schema field or broad roadmap entry does not establish complete, checked engineering capability.

Sources: [research](2026-09-09-agentic-tender-office-research.md), [office concept](2026-09-09-adaptive-ai-office-concept.md), [superseded foundation](../superpowers/plans/2026-09-09-office-staff-foundation.md), [accepted scope](../design/adaptive-office-scope.md), [master plan](../superpowers/plans/2026-09-09-adaptive-office-master.md), [current first-increment plan](../superpowers/plans/2026-09-09-dynamic-staff-live-office.md), [implementation report](dynamic-office-2026-09-10.md).

## What was under-specified in the execution plan

- The master plan's lifecycle paragraph assigns research catalogue items 1–72 to stages in large groups. It does not give each outcome an implementation owner, bounded task, required tool/data contract, acceptance fixture and measured completion status.
- The 50-assignment queue does not itself establish complete coverage. In particular, the later office refinement/learning/quality ideas and many specialist engineering outcomes are only implicit in broad assignments.
- The detailed communication lifecycle needs concrete contracts for delivery acknowledgement versus inspection/check/acceptance, ownership expiry/transfer, review challenges, change notices and circular waits. The current committed messages and source receipts are a foundation, not all of these states.
- Staff retirement/reactivation, full continuing notebooks, company method-pack activation, assumption expiry/impact links and measured staff quality need explicit delivery tasks.
- Personality consistency versus engineering competence, blind-review independence, collaboration overhead and comparison with a single well-equipped agent need distinct benchmark cases. General test counts do not answer these questions.
- Cost-aware staffing needs an explanation of what an additional reviewer is expected to resolve before more model work is spent. Shared budget enforcement does not provide that decision aid by itself.
- Optional library candidates need recorded adopt/defer/reject decisions based on representative documents, licences, platforms and capability gaps. Listing a library is not integrating or validating it.
- The concept's complete revised-drawing/team-review acceptance story has not been demonstrated. The implementation report covers a narrower synthetic office journey.
- Exact handoff references are saved, but a receiving staff execution does not yet have the Manager's `read_staff_result` tool. It cannot generally open another colleague's full saved staff-result payload through that tool boundary. Deliberate reuse of older-run staff results is also restricted rather than implemented as a revalidated transfer. This is a concrete gap in the concept's promise to share full workings, not merely summaries or IDs.

## Research features outside the numbered catalogue

- Market watchlists, comparable price histories, volatility/expiry/procurement-driven refresh, product equivalence, geographic/currency normalization, procurement timing, supplier coverage maps and concise market-change briefs need explicit methods/records.
- The four knowledge collections need enforced selection and retrieval semantics, with contextual expansion and contractual precedence rather than merely selecting the newest document.
- Staff meeting contributions need claim, evidence, method/calculation, uncertainty, impact and next action. Agreement alone must not count as an independent check.
- Assumptions need dependency/expiry conditions, and successful corrected work should become a proposed tested method rather than silently changing future behavior.
- The five research acceptance stories—review a package, check/price a BOQ, review an addendum, compare quotations, prepare a submission—need connected success and crash/cancellation/stale-source/permission variants. Current software suites do not establish all five with actual engineering inputs and independent expected answers.

## Library candidates not yet fully evaluated or integrated for these extensions

Docling, PaddleOCR, OCRmyPDF, Pint, Polars, DuckDB, SciPy, Shapely, ezdxf, a permitted ODA/licensed DWG route, OR-Tools, MPXJ, Pydantic Skills/deferred tools/Code Mode, Pydantic Evals/Ragas, and OpenTelemetry remain candidates requiring per-use-case decisions. Existing Python, Pydantic AI/provider SDKs, NumPy, FastEmbed, PDFium, openpyxl and python-docx should not be listed as wholly missing.

An independent search service (Tavily, Exa or Brave) and advanced page extraction/browser tooling (Firecrawl/Playwright) were research candidates, not a decision to install every service. Qdrant, a graph store, DBOS, Temporal, LangGraph and A2A were conditional architecture alternatives; omitting them is not itself a missing requirement. A2A is unnecessary for the local internal staff system unless an independently operated agent is actually integrated.

## Intentional exclusions and the superseded plan

The old staff-foundation file explicitly directs implementation to the dynamic-staff/live-office plan and contains no remaining fixed-roster task to execute. Restoring pre-created employees would violate the engineer's later instruction.

Ollama and the research section 11 business-system/portal/data-feed integration expansion are excluded. BIM/IFC is deferred. Connected tablet access and push-to-talk are later delivery choices. Public marketplaces, unrestricted execution, fine-tuning, extra orchestration frameworks and a generic ERP/site-management suite were not required for the current office increment.

The planning correction is to maintain item-level status and acceptance for the included outcomes, not to reinstall excluded components or claim every research library is mandatory. The first office increment is a real foundation; it is not completion of the full agentic Tender Office.
