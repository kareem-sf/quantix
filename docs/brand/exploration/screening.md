The screened category is saturated with blue/cyan gradients, hexagons, cubes, diamonds, loops, nodes, networks, arrows, interlocks, green workflow symbols, AI sparkles, document metaphors, and industrial uppercase wordmarks. The final Evidence Register deliberately rejects those saturated territories. Its closest generic reading is a QR/scanner frame; production refinement reduces that risk through the deliberately incomplete perimeter, uniform evidence cells, open lower-right boundary, and non-overlapping 37.7° datum with no arrowhead or check geometry.

## Supplied-direction screen

| Supplied direction | Decision | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Matrix Measure Loop | Advanced and reconstructed | Strongest link to registered evidence and Engineer authority; the source slash was rebuilt as a separated datum. |
| Infinite Ledger | Rejected | Segmented infinity reads crypto, blockchain, or generic continuous workflow and collapses at icon size. |
| Flowing Evidence | Rejected | Dot field plus Q is a common data-search and AI monogram construction. |
| Vector Infinity Grid | Rejected | Northeast arrow reads generic growth, export, and analytics; the loop becomes decorative. |
| Infinite Document Matrix | Rejected | Dense bilateral infinity reads middleware or automation and loses internal detail below 32 px. |
| Convergent Precision | Rejected | Central flare reads AI sparkle, crosshair, or optical focus. |
