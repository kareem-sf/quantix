# Quantix Evidence Register identity

The approved Archive Ink, Warm Vellum, Authority Copper, and Record Slate identity system remains intact. The symbol has been replaced by a production refinement of the supplied **Matrix Measure Loop** direction.

The Evidence Register expresses one Quantix relationship:

> Registered Tender Evidence remains exact until a Tendering Engineer deliberately crosses the authority boundary.

Canonical source files live in `src/assets/brand`:

- `quantix-mark.svg` — color Evidence Register for light surfaces.

`quantix-wordmark.svg` remains the custom-drawn wordmark. `comparison-board.html` documents the complete identity in app, icon, splash, bilingual, and small-size contexts. `screening.md` records preliminary marketplace screening.

## Construction

- 48×48 symbol grid.
- Ten 6×6 evidence cells on a 9-unit pitch.
- One open lower-right register boundary.
- One 37.7° closed Engineer datum, separated from every evidence cell.
- No text, hidden letters, arrows, checkmarks, gradients, filters, masks, or embedded raster artwork.
- Color is hierarchy only; the geometry remains legible in one color.

The datum is never described as AI. It identifies the human authority boundary. Quantix does not submit a Tender externally.

Use the symbol at ownership and orientation surfaces: application icon, transparent splash, sidebar, startup shell, About, installer, title bar, and notifications. Keep it out of Tender documents and routine evidence content.
