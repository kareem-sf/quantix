# Center each Tender on human-approved Manager Agent orchestration

Quantix identifies the human operator and sole formal approval authority as the Tendering Engineer, and the Tender-scoped lead AI as the Tendering Manager Agent. After restricted bootstrap analysis, the Manager Agent proposes the complete evidence-linked Work Plan and one exact `Approve and Proceed` decision binds the Bid Decision, Work Plan, approved authority envelope, team activation, and execution start; the Agent may then plan, delegate, coordinate, and recover routine work inside that envelope, while material amendments and later formal Tender decisions remain explicit Tendering Engineer approvals. This creates one accountable conversational relationship without turning chat or AI judgment into the Tender system of record.

This decision supersedes the human/AI role names and separate initial production-activation consequence in ADR 0001 and ADR 0005. Their remaining lifecycle, independence, versioning, evidence, safety, and EITL controls continue to apply.
