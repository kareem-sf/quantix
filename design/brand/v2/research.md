# Quantix identity redesign

Research date: 9 September 2026.

## Findings from the reference brands

**OpenAI / ChatGPT.** OpenAI documents fixed wordmark proportions, clear space, and a circular O. Its description of the Blossom combines circular, human qualities with the precision of right angles. Its primary-wordmark guidance excludes added effects and textures. These are specific OpenAI rules; the lesson for Quantix is to make the underlying letterforms and silhouette carry the identity. [Official design guidelines](https://openai.com/brand/).

**Anthropic.** Geist, the studio responsible for the original identity, describes a typographic logo with one distinctive slash. Its type pairing combined Styrene and Tiempos, while warmer colors supported both technical craft and a human focus. The useful method is a clear typographic foundation with one deliberate distinguishing detail. [Designer’s case study](https://geist.co/work/anthropic).

**xAI / Grok.** The official brand page distributes approved master logos but does not publish a geometric construction method or explain the logo’s symbolic origin. Those details should not be invented. In a separate account about Grok Bot avatars, the company describes rejecting approaches that lost clarity at sidebar size and using consistent simple construction. That account concerns avatars, not the Grok logo; it nevertheless provides a concrete example of designing for the actual display size. [Official brand assets](https://x.ai/legal/brand-guidelines), [Grok Bot design account](https://x.ai/news/designing-grok-bot).

## Design decisions for Quantix

These are our design conclusions, not rules attributed to the reference companies:

- Establish the master identity in one flat color, with clear negative space and consistent proportions.
- Give the name equal attention to the symbol. Use optical spacing and one distinctive construction idea.
- Compare three original directions: a continuous Q, a geometric junction, and a wordmark with an editorial character.
- Evaluate the marks on light and dark backgrounds and at icon size.
- Supply outlined SVG artwork, editable Blender geometry, and high-resolution images.
- Treat the 3D treatment as a separate presentation of the same master shape.

The reference companies’ artwork is not incorporated into the Quantix assets.
